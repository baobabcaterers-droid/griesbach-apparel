Our Business Model

ACES is a student-run clothing brand created for our Grade 7 Business Complementary course at Major General Griesbach School. Our goal is to provide high-quality everyday streetwear for middle school students at fair prices. Every item is locally designed with a focus on durability, comfort, and aesthetic minimalism.
