Business Plan

How ACES Works

ACES is a student-made clothing brand created for our Grade 7 Business Complementary course. Our idea is to sell simple, comfortable apparel through pre-orders so we only buy what customers actually want.

Target Customer

Students who want stylish clothing that is comfortable and not overpriced.

Our Value

Clean designs, fair prices, and a brand connected to our school community.

Sales Plan

Take class pre-orders, order in bulk, then deliver items at school.
